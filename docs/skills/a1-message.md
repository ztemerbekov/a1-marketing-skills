# A1 Message

**English** | [Русский](./a1-message.ru.md)

Use A1 Message when one existing business contact needs one clear workplace message built from supplied context or a selected draft.

## The job

A1 Message turns supplied workplace facts, notes, correspondence, documents, tables, or transcript material into one compact message for an existing business contact. It can also rewrite one selected workplace-message draft for the same source-supported communication job. The result keeps the recipient's current question visible without turning missing facts into confident wording.

## When to use it

Use it when a manager, colleague, employee, contractor, partner, or existing client needs to understand something, make a decision, approve a proposal, take an action, or reply. Start with an ordinary-language request or `/a1-message`.

Bring either the material from which the message should be assembled, or one selected workplace-message draft that needs to be rewritten for the same job. Use [A1 Decision Memo](./a1-decision-memo.md) for a layered internal decision document, [A1 Cold Email](./a1-cold-email.md) for first contact, and [A1 Copy Editing](./a1-copy-editing.md) for general editing or review of a selected marketing or editorial draft.

## What you get

One finished workplace message with the main point first, enough context to understand it, a source-supported recommendation or decision status, and an operational next step or an explicitly unresolved request. Material gaps such as a missing solution, owner, deadline, option, or approval remain visible; the message does not invent them.

> **Try asking:**
>
> `/a1-message`
>
> Use the campaign report and performance-contractor thread to write one message to Elon Musk explaining why a fictional campaign to recruit the first wave of Mars travelers missed its target. Lead with the cause, preserve the source-supported impact of pausing the ads and the status of the new creative assets, recommend a recovery plan grounded in the materials, and ask him to approve it by Friday at 4:00 p.m.

## Common questions

**Can it rewrite a draft?**

Yes, when the selected draft is a workplace message for an existing business contact and serves the same source-supported job. It can reorganize the reading path without adding facts or changing the decision status.

**Can it summarize forwarded correspondence?**

Yes. It turns the correspondence into a short, conclusion-bearing update with the details the recipient needs instead of pasting the raw thread.

**What happens when the sources contain no solution or deadline?**

The message keeps that absence explicit and asks only for a source-supported decision, diagnostic input, or assignment. It does not fill the gap with a plausible plan or date.

**Where does a long memo or a cold first contact belong?**

Use [A1 Decision Memo](./a1-decision-memo.md) for a layered internal decision document and [A1 Cold Email](./a1-cold-email.md) for first contact with someone with whom no prior conversation exists.

## How to recognize success

- The main point and the response, decision, action, or reply needed from the recipient appear first.
- Recommendations, proposals, and decision status are distinguishable and supported by the supplied material.
- Necessary context and real anchors such as names, dates, amounts, or identifiers are easy to scan.
- The next step is operational and source-supported, or the unresolved gap is stated plainly.
- The recipient can understand, decide, act, or reply without avoidable follow-up.
