# A1 Decision Memo

**English** | [Русский](./a1-decision-memo.ru.md)

## What it does

A1 Decision Memo turns supplied reports, correspondence, notes, analysis, or chronology into one internal document for a specific reader. It puts current status and the required decision, response, or explicit no-action state first, then preserves enough structured detail for verification.

The skill organizes specialist input; it does not silently redo legal, financial, technical, or strategic analysis or invent the decision.

## When to use it

Use it for an approval memo, project-status memo, executive brief, recommendation memo, escalation note, or another internal document whose reader must decide, approve, respond, or understand current status. The skill is model-invoked for that complete intent and may also be invoked as `/a1-decision-memo`.

Use [A1 Copy Editing](./a1-copy-editing.md) when a selected finished memo only needs editing or review. Use [A1 Article Writing](./a1-article-writing.md) for public long-form content.

## Reader-ready structure

The result leads with an executive layer that can stand alone, followed by conclusion-first sections, options and tradeoffs when supplied, risks and unknowns, next steps, and background detail only where the material needs them.

Marketing Context is optional and never replaces the memo's source pack.

## Common questions

**Will it choose an option for me?**

Only when the supplied recommendation or decision criteria establish that conclusion. Otherwise it presents the options and names the unresolved criterion.

**Does it have to shorten the source?**

No. The memo is layered rather than merely short: detail remains available without blocking the top line.

**Can it organize legal or financial material?**

Yes, as completed external input. It preserves specialist meaning and attribution but does not replace professional analysis or approval.

## It's working if

- The reader can identify the current state, significance, and whether any decision, response, or action is required from the opening.
- Facts, assessments, options, recommendations, and decisions cannot be confused.
- Each section starts with its conclusion or decision significance.
- Chronology and technical detail remain available without controlling the reading path.
- Missing owners, dates, criteria, or approvals remain visible rather than being invented.

## Where it fits

A1 Decision Memo owns internal documents for a decision, response, or awareness-only update. A1 Copy Editing owns editorial changes to a selected finished document; A1 Article Writing owns public or educational long-form work.
