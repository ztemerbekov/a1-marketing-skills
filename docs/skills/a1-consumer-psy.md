# A1 Consumer Psychology

**English** | [Русский](./a1-consumer-psy.ru.md)

Use A1 Consumer Psychology when you need to know whether a behavioral explanation is scientifically defensible and what evidence would support or weaken it before you change marketing or product decisions.

## The job

You have a consumer-psychology hypothesis to audit before launch, or a completed marketing test whose result needs an explanation. You want to separate what was observed from what is merely plausible, identify the smallest defensible mechanism set, and choose the next useful test.

The skill does not turn a familiar bias name into a causal finding. It works from a reviewed local research catalog, shows credible alternatives and limits, and treats every research effect as something that still needs local verification.

## When to use it

Invoke the skill deliberately with `/a1-consumer-psy`; it does not start automatically. Supply the observed behavior, interface or message, test design, outcome, and any mechanism you already suspect.

Do not use it instead of traffic analysis, attribution, instrumentation review, or technical diagnosis. Use [A1 Grill](./a1-grill.md) when the job is to challenge a broader marketing decision rather than evaluate a behavioral mechanism.

## What you get

A completed diagnosis separates observations, interpretations, and unknowns; names a leading hypothesis only when the facts favor it; explains alternatives and scientific limits; and gives one decision-useful next step. Proposed verification includes a falsifiable prediction, primary metric, countermetrics, possible backfire, and a result that would weaken the hypothesis.

When an intervention creates material risk, the result explains the risk in plain language and adds a truthful, reversible alternative. The skill never fabricates popularity, authority, scarcity, urgency, reviews, prices, consent, or user choice.

## Common questions

**Can it tell me the definitive reason conversion changed?**

No. It can identify defensible hypotheses and the smallest test that would distinguish them. It cannot replace missing analytics, attribution, or technical diagnosis.

**Do I need to know a model name?**

No. For an unnamed diagnosis, the skill selects the smallest relevant set from the supported catalog. If you name a covered model, it can explain that model directly.

**Will it recommend manipulative tactics?**

It assesses risk before proposing an intervention. Material risk receives a warning, a truthful alternative, and safeguards. Fabricated claims and hidden material conditions remain outside the boundary.

## How to recognize success

- Observations, interpretations, and unknowns are clearly separated.
- Every retained mechanism fits a defining operation in the case.
- Credible alternatives and limits on local transfer remain visible.
- Proposed tests can weaken the hypothesis and include countermetrics.
- The decision status says whether evidence supports action, requires more evidence, or does not support the hypothesis.
