# Cold-Email Source Policy

Use only:

1. Recipient, sender, offer, purpose, and next-step facts supplied in the current request.
2. User-selected completed prospect research, profiles, correspondence, product documentation, approved proof, or examples.
3. Clearly applicable facts supplied earlier in the conversation.
4. The first eligible repository Marketing Context selected by `SKILL.md`.

Treat a claimed fit as a hypothesis unless the sources establish it. Do not invent a recipient role, priority, pain, recent event, mutual connection, compliment, quotation, result, client, credential, price, deadline, or referral.

Use completed research as supplied input; this skill does not conduct prospect research. When the material supports only a possible fit, preserve that uncertainty in the email instead of converting it into confident personalization.

Connect sender capability to a recipient outcome only when the sources support the mechanism or evidence. Keep unresolved details outside the publishable email unless the user explicitly asks for a marked working template.
