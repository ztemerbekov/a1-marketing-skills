# Cold-Email Spine

Every completed run follows these stages in order.

## 1. Confirm the Contact Job

Identify the recipient, sender, reason for contact, relevant offer, and smallest useful response. Confirm that no prior conversation makes the message warm.

Ask one concise question only when every credible email would otherwise invent the recipient, relevance, offer, sender identity, or next step.

**Complete when:** one recipient, one contact reason, and one proportionate response are clear.

## 2. Build the Source Map

Apply [source-policy.md](source-policy.md). Separate recipient facts, the real point of connection, sender capability, offer facts, proof, uncertainty, and missing material.

**Complete when:** every specific statement the email may use is traceable and unsupported personalization is excluded.

## 3. Establish Relevance

Find the narrowest supported answer to `Why this recipient, from this sender, now?` Use an actual connection, observed situation, completed request, or credible fit signal. Acknowledge possible misfit when the available evidence is incomplete.

Avoid ceremonial praise, manufactured familiarity, and generic observations that could address any recipient.

**Complete when:** the opening can establish relevance without pretending to have a relationship.

## 4. Build the Value Case

State the recipient-relevant use of the offer before sender history. Connect capability to a supported consequence, example, or proof item. Keep implementation detail for a later exchange unless it is necessary to judge fit.

**Complete when:** the recipient can see a plausible reason to reply without accepting a stronger claim than the sources support.

## 5. Draft the Email

Write two to five short paragraphs. Use a specific, honest subject line. Move through grounded relevance, contact reason, useful offer, material proof or limitation, and one low-cost next step.

The next step should be cheaper than a meeting when a short reply, permission to send an example, or confirmation of fit would move the conversation forward. Do not disguise a sales message with `Re:`, `Fwd:`, false urgency, or a fabricated referral.

**Complete when:** the email is personal, compact, credible, and easy to answer.

## 6. Run Final QA

Revise until all applicable checks pass:

- the message could not be sent unchanged to a large unrelated list;
- every recipient and sender fact is sourced;
- the value case centers on recipient use rather than sender enthusiasm;
- uncertainty and fit limits remain visible;
- the requested response is specific, proportionate, and truthful;
- the email contains no invented relationship, praise, pain, proof, deadline, or scarcity.

**Complete when:** the final email earns attention through relevance and makes a reply easier than ignoring an unclear request.
