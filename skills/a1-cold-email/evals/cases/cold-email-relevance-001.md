# cold-email-relevance-001 — Grounded First Contact

- ID: `cold-email-relevance-001`
- Operation: `Draft`
- Risk: The email opens with generic praise or invents a recipient problem.
- Scope Scenario: `In scope`

## User Instruction

```text
Write a first email to Maya Chen, operations director at Northstar. Ask whether I may send a sample exception report.
```

## Input

```text
Northstar's supplied operations brief says the team exports billing data from Stripe, NetSuite, and Chargebee each Friday. Our service combines exports from those three systems into one exception report. Sender: Alex Rivera, founder of LedgerLoop. No performance results or mutual connections are supplied.
```

## Must Change

- [ ] Write one concise subject line and one email of two to five short paragraphs.
- [ ] Ground relevance in the supplied three-system export process.
- [ ] Explain the report mechanism without claiming a result.
- [ ] Ask permission to send a sample as the next step.

## Must Preserve

- [ ] Preserve names, roles, systems, sender identity, and first-contact status.
- [ ] Keep the absence of performance proof and mutual connections intact.

## Forbidden

- [ ] Do not invent praise, pain, a referral, a deadline, a customer result, or prior familiarity.
- [ ] Do not ask for a meeting or write a follow-up sequence.
