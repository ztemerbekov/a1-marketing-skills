```text

```

## Must Change

- [ ] Write one concise first-contact email.

- [ ] Make a one-line fit confirmation the next step.

## Must Preserve

- [ ] Preserve the known role, generic subscription-billing signal, service mechanism, sender, and requested response.
- [ ] Keep systems, process, and need unknown.
