# Press-Release Final QA

Run this check against the complete result.

## Scope and News

- The request is a routine announcement within `../SKILL.md`.
- One concrete event anchors the release.
- The angle states source-supported relevance without inflated novelty.
- The release does not drift into crisis, legal, regulatory, earnings, securities, or investor-relations communication.

## Verification

- The result's readiness matches the [Readiness Map](source-policy.md#readiness-map).
- Every name, title, date, time, location, figure, link, partnership, claim, and quotation traces to [source-policy.md](source-policy.md).
- Approved facts and proposed wording are visibly distinct.
- Weekdays, time zones, deadlines, eligibility, prices, and links are internally consistent.
- Unresolved material remains outside distribution-ready copy.

## Media Usefulness

- The headline states the event and useful consequence.
- The lead works without prior company context.
- Body paragraphs provide distinct, reusable facts.
- Any approved quote adds perspective rather than ceremony; any proposed wording appears only in a working draft, was explicitly requested, and is unmistakably marked for approval.
- Action, boilerplate, contact, and assets appear when relevant and supplied.

## Prose and Tone

- The release is natural and uses newsroom-friendly syntax.
- Corporate ceremony, unsupported superlatives, and empty self-praise are absent.
- Technical and institutional terms remain accurate and understandable for the intended media audience.
- Attribution cannot be mistaken.

## Output Integrity

- The response follows `../SKILL.md`.
- The separate pre-distribution checklist contains only material checks.
- Artifact-only requests still communicate their actual readiness and never hide required checks.

Revise until every applicable check passes. If the event itself or a distribution-critical fact remains unresolved, return a clearly labeled working draft or request the missing fact rather than presenting the release as ready to send.
