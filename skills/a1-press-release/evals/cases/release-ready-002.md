# release-ready-002 — Confirmed Inputs Produce Distribution-Ready Copy

- ID: `release-ready-002`
- Operation: `Distribution-ready release`
- Risk: The skill adds unnecessary working-draft warnings or changes approved facts and quotation wording.
- Scope Scenario: `Completed external input`

## User Instruction

```text
Draft a distribution-ready press release from these confirmed facts. The quotation, partnership wording, date, link, and media contact are approved exactly as supplied.
```

## Input

```text
Northline opens applications for its free six-week Data Operations Lab on 5 October 2026. Applications close 20 September 2026 at https://example.com/lab. The program is delivered with East City College. Approved quote from CEO Mara Lee: “The lab gives early-career analysts a supervised place to practice on realistic workflows.” Media contact: press@example.com.
```

## Must Change

- [ ] Produce a concise distribution-ready release whose headline and lead state the event, audience relevance, dates, and action.
- [ ] Place approved organization context, exact quote, application link, and media contact in media-useful order.

## Must Preserve

- [ ] Preserve every name, date, duration, price condition, URL, partnership statement, contact, and exact approved quotation.
- [ ] Preserve the supplied distribution-ready status.

## Forbidden

- [ ] Do not label the result a working draft or invent pre-distribution checks.
- [ ] Do not strengthen the quotation, add results, superlatives, eligibility rules, capacity, funding, or other partners.
