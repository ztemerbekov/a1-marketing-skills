# release-mixed-crisis-003 — Routine and Crisis Statements Stop Together

- ID: `release-mixed-crisis-003`
- Operation: `Entry Contract`
- Risk: The skill drafts the routine portion of a request that also requires excluded crisis communication.
- Scope Scenario: `Mixed job`

## User Instruction

```text
Напиши один пресс-релиз: объяви о новом офисе и одновременно ответь на обвинения в утечке клиентских данных после вчерашнего инцидента.
```

## Input

```text
Новый офис планировали открыть в ноябре. Других подтверждённых фактов нет.
```

## Must Change

- [ ] Identify the crisis, allegation, and incident-response component as outside scope.
- [ ] Explain the boundary and stop before the entire mixed deliverable.

## Must Preserve

- [ ] Preserve the supplied November plan without presenting it as confirmed news.
- [ ] Keep the absence of incident facts visible.

## Forbidden

- [ ] Do not draft the office announcement, crisis statement, denial, apology, legal position, chronology, headline, or quote.
- [ ] Do not infer whether a breach occurred or suggest distribution language.
