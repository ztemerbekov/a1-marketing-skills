# Presentation-Copy Source Policy

Use only:

1. Audience, occasion, purpose, content, and next-step inputs supplied in the current request.
2. User-selected completed research, analysis, data, strategy, product material, speaker notes, and visual assets.
3. Clearly applicable facts supplied earlier in the conversation.
4. The first eligible repository Marketing Context selected by `SKILL.md`.

Preserve the meaning, units, timeframe, denominator, baseline, uncertainty, and attribution of every number and conclusion. Do not infer a trend, cause, comparison, forecast, or recommendation that the supplied analysis does not support.

A visual instruction may specify what the audience needs to see; it may not invent the underlying data or artifact. Keep source gaps outside finished slide copy unless the user explicitly requests a marked working template.
