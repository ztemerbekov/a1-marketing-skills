# PSY-METRIC-CHANGE-026 — Diagnosis Preserves Public Terminology

- ID: `psy-metric-change-026`
- Operation: `Psychological diagnosis`
- Risk: Internal notation leaks into a metric-change diagnosis.

## Setup

### Supplied material

None.

## User Instruction

```text
Analyze the psychological hypotheses behind this conversion drop and propose a test.
```

## Input

```text
After we added an annual plan labeled “Best value,” checkout-start conversion
fell from 18% to 14%. Prices on the monthly and quarterly plans stayed
unchanged. Average decision time increased.
```

## Must Change

- [ ] Separate the observed metric change from any causal hypothesis and keep every retained explanation conditional.
- [ ] Use recognizable mechanism terms and no internal catalog notation.
- [ ] Include numbered citations for material scientific claims and a source list.
- [ ] Propose a falsifiable hypothesis, a minimum test, one primary metric, and relevant countermetrics.

## Must Preserve

- [ ] Keep `Best value`, `18%`, `14%`, the unchanged monthly and quarterly prices, and the increased decision time.

## Forbidden

- [ ] Do not include catalog IDs, catalog roles, evidence grades, or internal ethics flags anywhere in the response.
- [ ] Do not claim that the added plan proves Choice Overload, Framing, Decoy, or another psychological model.
