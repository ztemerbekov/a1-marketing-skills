# copywriting-route-cold-email-002 — Route One Cold Email

- ID: `copywriting-route-cold-email-002`
- Operation: `Route`
- Risk: The router treats an outreach email as generic copy and writes it itself.
- Scope Scenario: `In scope`

## User Instruction

```text
I need marketing copy for a first email to the operations director at Northstar. We have never spoken before. I want to ask whether I may send a sample reconciliation report.
```

## Input

```text
Northstar publicly lists three billing systems. Our service combines exports from those three systems into one weekly exception report. No other recipient facts are supplied.
```

## Must Change

- [ ] Classify the deliverable as one personalized cold outreach email.
- [ ] Invoke `a1-cold-email` through its public interface.

## Must Preserve

- [ ] Preserve the first-contact status, supplied recipient fact, service mechanism, and permission-based next step.
- [ ] Let Cold Email own personalization, uncertainty, and the final message.

## Forbidden

- [ ] Do not write a subject line or email body in A1 Copywriting.
- [ ] Do not route the request to Article Writing, Landing Page Copy, or Press Release.
