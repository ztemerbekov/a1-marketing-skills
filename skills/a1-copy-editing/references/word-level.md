# Word Level

Edit words as carriers of meaning, not as a blacklist. A filler, introduction, intensifier, jargon term, or cliché is a problem only when it obscures, delays, exaggerates, or replaces useful information.

## Meaning Test

For a suspicious word or phrase:

1. Identify the fact, relationship, limitation, certainty, rhythm, or voice it contributes.
2. Remove it mentally.
3. If the meaning and useful voice remain, cut it.
4. If removal exposes a missing fact, add specificity only from allowed sources.
5. If the sources do not provide specificity, keep honest uncertainty or remove the unsupported claim.

## Empty Introductions

Phrases such as `It is important to understand`, `It should be noted`, `Needless to say`, `Следует отметить`, `Важно понимать`, and `Как известно` often postpone the point.

Begin with the information itself. Keep an introductory phrase when it accurately signals evidence status, contrast, or the author's relationship to the statement.

## Intensifiers

Words such as `very`, `extremely`, `absolutely`, `truly`, `incredibly`, `максимально`, `совершенно`, and `невероятно` often add evaluation without evidence.

- Remove the intensifier when the claim is unchanged.
- Replace it with a sourced measurement, condition, or consequence when available.
- Keep it when it carries meaningful voice and does not impersonate proof.

## Vague Quantity and Scope

Words such as `many`, `various`, `some`, `approximately`, `often`, `ряд`, `многие`, and `достаточно` may be necessary uncertainty or empty scope.

Use a number, group, example, frequency, or condition when the sources provide one. Otherwise preserve honest uncertainty rather than inventing precision.

## Jargon and Bureaucratic Vocabulary

A technical term is justified when it is more precise than a simple substitute and appropriate for the reader. Explain it when the source supports an explanation and the intended reader needs one.

Simplify inflated wording by meaning, not by dictionary substitution:

- `in order to` or `в целях` may become `to` or `чтобы`;
- `at the present time` or `в настоящее время` may become `now` or disappear;
- a light verb plus nominalization may become one direct verb;
- `is/serves as/является` may become a direct relation when grammar allows.

Do not replace a legal, scientific, product, or professional term with a looser everyday word that changes scope.

## Unsupported Evaluation

Claims such as `best`, `innovative`, `reliable`, `seamless`, `professional`, `unique`, `качественный`, `эффективный`, and `передовой` are weak without support.

Replace evaluation with an observable sourced fact, mechanism, consequence, or limit. Remove it when no proof exists.

## Clichés and Generic Brand Language

Phrases such as `team of professionals`, `tailored approach`, `wide range of services`, `in today's fast-changing world`, `команда профессионалов`, and `индивидуальный подход` make different organizations sound interchangeable.

Use the actual action, process, choice, qualification, or constraint from the source. When the source contains none, omission is stronger than a new slogan.

## Transition Markers

Words such as `Furthermore`, `Moreover`, `Additionally`, `Кроме того`, and `Более того` should express a real relationship. Do not use them as automatic paragraph starters.

Begin with the subject, action, condition, or conclusion when the relationship is already clear.

## Completion Check

Word-level work is complete when every remaining word contributes meaning, precision, evidence status, navigation, rhythm, or authentic voice.
