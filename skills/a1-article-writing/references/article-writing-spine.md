# Article-Writing Spine

Every completed run follows these stages in order.

## 1. Classify the Job

Confirm that the requested outcome is one new long-form article or its outline. Select Outline or Draft and record explicit constraints on length, format, audience, voice, sources, and delivery.

**Complete when:** the article job, output mode, and neighboring-skill boundary are unambiguous.

## 2. Build the Source Map

Read [source-policy.md](source-policy.md). Separate evidence, experience, quotations, interpretation, and gaps. Resolve a material source conflict before writing; preserve harmless uncertainty.

**Complete when:** every specific element allowed in the result has a traceable source and unsupported central claims are excluded or surfaced.

## 3. Set the Editorial Brief

Determine from the request and sources:

- the reader and the situation in which they encounter the article;
- the useful outcome: what the reader should understand, decide, or be able to do;
- the article's angle and depth;
- the publication format and reading conditions;
- the evidence that can fulfill the promise.

Do not force a setup interview when the material already answers these questions. Ask only for a missing input that would make every direction materially arbitrary or unsupported.

**Complete when:** one sentence can state who the article is for, what it helps them accomplish, and what evidence makes that possible.

## 4. Choose the Structure

Read [structure-and-depth.md](structure-and-depth.md). Choose a shape from the material and reader job rather than applying a universal template. Build informative headings and assign each section one purpose and its supporting material.

In Outline mode, skip drafting after checking that the structure can fulfill the article's promise, then continue to final QA.

**Complete when:** each section advances the reader outcome, evidence has a place, and no section exists only to imitate a familiar article format.

## 5. Draft

Write the article in the requested voice. Start with useful content, preserve source qualifications, explain through concrete cases or mechanisms, and make headings independently informative.

Keep product mentions proportional to their actual role. A company article should remain useful even to a reader who does not buy, unless the user explicitly requested promotional content and should instead use `a1-landing-page-copy`.

**Complete when:** the article fulfills its opening promise with source-supported material and the reader can follow or scan it without reconstructing the author's notes.

## 6. Run Final QA

Run [final-qa.md](final-qa.md) and revise until every applicable check passes.

**Complete when:** the final draft is credible, useful, structurally coherent, and compliant with the output contract in `../SKILL.md`.
