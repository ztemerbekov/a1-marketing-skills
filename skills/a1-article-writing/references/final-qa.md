# Article Final QA

Run this check against the complete result.

## Source Integrity

- Every specific fact, quotation, number, date, example, result, and causal statement is traceable under [source-policy.md](source-policy.md).
- Uncertainty, attribution, scale, and material conditions remain visible.
- Proposed copy is not presented as an approved quotation or verified result.
- Links and citations support the claims beside them.

## Reader Outcome

- The article serves one identifiable reader situation and fulfills a useful outcome.
- The opening begins that work immediately.
- The promised scope matches the material actually delivered.
- Product or author promotion does not displace the reader's job.

## Structure and Depth

- The chosen structure follows the material rather than a default formula.
- Every section has one purpose, a useful heading, and enough support.
- Examples, mechanisms, implementation, limits, or tradeoffs provide the depth appropriate to the claim.
- Selective readers can recover the argument from headings and key elements when the format calls for scanning.

## Prose and Voice

- The prose is natural and idiomatic.
- Tone matches the reader relationship, subject, channel, and supplied voice.
- Technical terms are retained when useful and explained when the intended reader would need help.
- Grammar, collocation, references, and transitions are correct and unambiguous.

## Output Integrity

- Outline and Draft modes follow `../SKILL.md`.
- Editorial notes describe decisions actually made.
- Evidence gaps appear only when material and stay outside publishable copy.
- Artifact-only requests contain only the requested artifact.

Revise until every applicable check passes. If a central gap cannot be repaired without new evidence, return the strongest honest partial result and name the missing source rather than manufacturing completion.
