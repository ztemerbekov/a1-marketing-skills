# Framework and analyst guardrails

These twelve positions are **support** positions. They never become ordinary primary or secondary psychological causes. Use them to route analysis, reject a false synonym, identify an analyst error, or explain a named framework. The [evidence ledger](evidence-ledger.md) owns grades and sources.

## M01 — Correspondence Bias / Fundamental Attribution Error

- **Definition:** over-attribute behavior to a person's disposition while underweighting situational constraints.
- **Boundary:** not a generic causal explanation for customer behavior or every actor–observer difference.
- **Destination:** ask which situational variables are unobserved; route to a tested behavioral mechanism only when its operation is present.

## M23 — Commitment and Consistency

- **Definition:** a family of commitment-linked behavior-change operations.
- **Boundary:** does not merge public commitment, self-signaling, dissonance, low-ball, or consistency preference.
- **Destination:** identify the exact commitment operation; route to a narrower active model when one is supported.

## M37 — Hick–Hyman Law

- **Definition:** relation between information/choice alternatives and choice reaction time in constrained tasks.
- **Boundary:** not a law of conversion, UX quality, choice overload, or satisfaction.
- **Destination:** use only for reaction-time measurement with mapping, practice, and task-structure conditions; otherwise route to a relevant choice model or no primary.

## M38 — Nudge / Choice Architecture

- **Definition:** an intervention class that changes a choice environment through heterogeneous mechanisms.
- **Boundary:** not one causal psychological mechanism and not evidence that a particular intervention works locally.
- **Destination:** identify the active operation (for example, a default or information display) and evaluate that model directly.

## M41 — COM-B

- **Definition:** a capability–opportunity–motivation diagnostic framework.
- **Boundary:** framework classification or prediction is not causal intervention evidence.
- **Destination:** use it to organize missing inputs, then route to a named mechanism or test; do not report COM-B as the cause.

## M42 — Survivorship Bias

- **Definition:** selection/collider error from reasoning only from observed survivors or selected cases.
- **Boundary:** not publication bias and not a psychological mechanism of users.
- **Destination:** inspect the selection rule and missing cases before psychological diagnosis.

## N02 — Intention–Behavior Gap

- **Definition:** empirical regularity in which measured intention does not fully translate into later matched behavior.
- **Boundary:** not weak willpower or a self-contained causal mechanism.
- **Destination:** measure capability, opportunity, memory, habit, friction, and context; route to a discriminating active mechanism or no primary.

## N14 — Reference-Dependent Choice

- **Definition:** theory family in which valuation depends on a reference point.
- **Boundary:** not a single rule for reference formation or a substitute for loss aversion, anchoring, reference price, or context effects.
- **Destination:** identify the specific reference operation and route to its narrower model.

## N21 — Implementation Friction

- **Definition:** action cost or difficulty dimension such as time, search, money, motor effort, uncertainty, or cognitive cost.
- **Boundary:** not one common mechanism, dose, or welfare direction.
- **Destination:** specify the friction subtype and test it; do not treat “friction” as a circular cause.

## N23 — Signaling Theory

- **Definition:** formal account of information asymmetry in which signals can separate types when mimicry costs differ.
- **Boundary:** a badge, claim, or visible behavior is not automatically a credible signal.
- **Destination:** inspect sender/receiver, hidden quality, cost, and differential mimicry; otherwise route to source credibility or another active mechanism.

## N25 — Theory of Planned Behavior

- **Definition:** predictive framework connecting attitudes, norms, perceived control, intention, and behavior.
- **Boundary:** predictive association and framework application are not causal intervention results.
- **Destination:** use it to locate measured predictors and route an intervention to a specific mechanism; keep objective behavior and horizon explicit.

## N26 — Elaboration Likelihood Model

- **Definition:** persuasion framework about processing conditions and argument/cue effects.
- **Boundary:** a message cue is not proof of a central or peripheral route, and route labels are not causal evidence.
- **Destination:** measure processing and argument conditions, then test the actual message mechanism without promising attitude or behavior transfer.
