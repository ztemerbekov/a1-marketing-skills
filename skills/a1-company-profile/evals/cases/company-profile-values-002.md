# company-profile-values-002 — Values Through Practice

- ID: `company-profile-values-002`
- Operation: `Draft`
- Risk: The profile turns confirmed policies into unsupported virtue claims.
- Scope Scenario: `In scope`

## User Instruction

```text
Write a short company profile that includes our approved value of repair before replacement.
```

## Input

```text
North Mill repairs commercial kitchen equipment. Its approved service policy requires technicians to document a repair option before recommending replacement. Customers receive the diagnosis, repair estimate, replacement estimate, and expected service life of each option. The company serves restaurants within 80 miles of Bristol. No environmental-impact measurements are available.
```

## Must Change

- [ ] Explain the company, audience, geography, and service.
- [ ] Express repair before replacement through the supplied technician and estimate practices.

## Must Preserve

- [ ] Preserve the approved value, documentation policy, customer deliverables, service area, and absence of impact data.

## Forbidden

- [ ] Do not call the company sustainable, ethical, green, leading, or environmentally beneficial.
- [ ] Do not invent waste, emissions, savings, or customer results.
