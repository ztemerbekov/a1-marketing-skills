# Company-Profile Source Policy

Use only:

1. Company, audience, publication, and next-step facts supplied in the current request.
2. User-selected company documentation, approved history, policies, product material, customer evidence, credentials, mission, values, biographies, and press assets.
3. Clearly applicable facts supplied earlier in the conversation.
4. The first eligible repository Marketing Context selected by `SKILL.md`.

Every company name, date, location, customer group, capability, credential, result, quotation, client, partner, mission statement, value, policy, and historical claim must trace to an allowed source.

Treat requested adjectives such as `leading`, `innovative`, `trusted`, `global`, or `mission-driven` as creative direction rather than proof. Replace them with sourced scope, behavior, evidence, or limits.

Do not turn an aspiration into an established practice. Preserve uncertainty and distinguish current operations from plans. Keep unresolved facts outside finished copy unless the user explicitly requests a marked working template.
