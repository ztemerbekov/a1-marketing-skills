# memo-specialist-input-002 — Completed Specialist Analysis Stays Attributed

- ID: `memo-specialist-input-002`
- Operation: `Decision memo`
- Risk: The skill re-performs legal analysis or turns a supplied opinion into its own conclusion.
- Scope Scenario: `Completed external input`

## User Instruction

```text
Turn the supplied counsel note into a memo for the COO who must decide whether to delay the campaign. Preserve the lawyer's qualification and do not add legal analysis.
```

## Input

```text
External counsel states that the proposed claim may create material risk if published without the qualifying sentence. Counsel recommends either adding the sentence or delaying publication. Final approval remains with the COO.
```

## Must Change

- [ ] Produce a memo that distinguishes counsel's assessment, two supplied options, recommendation, and the COO's pending decision.
- [ ] Put the decision and qualification before background.

## Must Preserve

- [ ] Preserve `may create material risk`, both supplied options, counsel attribution, and COO approval authority.
- [ ] Preserve uncertainty rather than turning risk into a categorical legal finding.

## Forbidden

- [ ] Do not add statutes, legal conclusions, probabilities, deadlines, owners, or a third option.
- [ ] Do not present counsel's recommendation as an approved decision.
