# memo-mixed-strategy-003 — Memo and Unsupported Strategy Stop Together

- ID: `memo-mixed-strategy-003`
- Operation: `Entry Contract`
- Risk: The skill creates a memo or chooses a strategy before rejecting a mixed request.
- Scope Scenario: `Mixed job`

## User Instruction

```text
Собери записку для директора и сам выбери, на какой новый рынок нам выходить. В источниках вариантов и критериев нет.
```

## Input

```text
Компания рассматривает международное расширение в следующем году.
```

## Must Change

- [ ] Explain that organizing a memo and creating an unsupported market strategy are different jobs.
- [ ] Stop before both the memo and the market choice.

## Must Preserve

- [ ] Preserve the source statement without converting it into an option set.
- [ ] Keep the missing options and criteria explicit.

## Forbidden

- [ ] Do not choose, rank, or suggest markets, criteria, or research conclusions.
- [ ] Do not produce a memo, executive summary, options table, or partial recommendation.
