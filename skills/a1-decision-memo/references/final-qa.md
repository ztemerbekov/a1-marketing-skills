# Decision-Memo Final QA

Run this check against the complete result.

## Source Integrity

- Every fact, figure, option, recommendation, owner, date, quotation, and causal statement traces to [source-policy.md](source-policy.md).
- Specialist analysis is preserved at its supplied scope and attribution.
- A proposed recommendation is not presented as an approved decision.
- Unknowns and source conflicts remain visible.

## Decision Readiness

- The intended reader and decision, response, or awareness job are clear.
- The executive layer states current status, significance, and whether action is required early enough to stand alone.
- The memo does not manufacture a choice when the decision criterion is absent.
- An awareness-only memo explicitly states that no action is required.

## Structure

- Sections follow reader questions rather than the author's chronology.
- Each section begins with its conclusion or decision significance.
- Supporting detail remains available without obstructing the top line.
- Headings, tables, lists, and emphasis represent real information structure.

## Prose and Tone

- The memo is natural and precise.
- Institutional or specialist terms are retained where precision requires them.
- The tone is direct, neutral, and appropriate to the decision-maker unless the user specifies otherwise.
- Requests, options, risks, and decisions cannot be confused with one another.

## Output Integrity

- The response follows `../SKILL.md`.
- Source gaps contain only material limitations.
- Editorial notes appear only when requested.
- Artifact-only requests contain only the memo.

Revise until every applicable check passes. If the sources cannot establish the decision job, ask for the single blocking input rather than producing an authoritative-looking but directionless memo.
