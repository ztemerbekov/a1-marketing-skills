# Decision-Memo Spine

Every completed run follows these stages in order.

## 1. Classify the Job

Identify the intended decision-maker, the decision, response, or awareness this document should enable, and the supplied source pack. Record explicit constraints on format, length, confidentiality markings, and required headings.

If the reader or the memo's decision, response, or awareness job is not named, infer it only when the material makes one interpretation clearly safer than the alternatives. Otherwise ask one concise question.

**Complete when:** the reader, decision, response, or awareness job, source pack, and output constraint are unambiguous.

## 2. Build the Decision Map

Read [source-policy.md](source-policy.md). Separate facts, assessments, options, recommendations, decisions, requests, risks, owners, deadlines, and supporting detail.

**Complete when:** every substantive element allowed in the memo is traceable and unsupported choices are excluded.

## 3. Design the Reading Path

Read [decision-structure.md](decision-structure.md). Put the current conclusion and any requested action before chronology; when no action is required, make that clear in the executive layer. Group details by the questions the reader may need to inspect.

Choose only the sections the material needs. Preserve a user-required template, but make each section conclusion-first within that template.

**Complete when:** the top of the memo can stand alone and the body lets the reader verify or deepen one issue without reading everything.

## 4. Draft the Memo

Write the executive layer first, then the supporting body. Use informative headings and open each section with its conclusion, current state, or decision significance. Move chronology, process history, and source detail below the point they explain.

Do not simplify specialist terms when simplification would change meaning. Explain a term only when the sources support the explanation and the intended reader needs it.

**Complete when:** the reader can identify what happened, why it matters, whether action is requested, and what remains uncertain.

## 5. Run Final QA

Run [final-qa.md](final-qa.md) and revise until every applicable check passes.

**Complete when:** the memo is clear about its decision, response, or awareness job; source-faithful; appropriately detailed; and compliant with the output contract in `../SKILL.md`.
