# Editor Brief

The Editor Brief is the internal handoff contract from `a1-editor-in-chief` to `a1-editor`. Keep it hidden unless the user explicitly asks to inspect it.

## Template

```markdown
## Editor Brief

**Source material:** [exact supplied text or task material]
**Source precedence and conflicts:** [winning current sources and any material conflict]
**Reader:** [confirmed or safely resolved reader state]
**Goal:** [what the text must help the reader understand, believe, or do]
**Channel or format:** [confirmed or safely resolved]
**Constraints:** [facts, claims, voice, length, legal, format, and protected elements]
**Confirmed message:** [message supplied by an allowed source]
**Editorial angle:** [bounded angle on the confirmed message]
**Argument hierarchy:** [order and emphasis of existing arguments]
**Structure:** [editorial structure]
**Voice:** [voice decision]
**Primary Editor operation:** [one operation label]
**Allowed moves:** [what Editor may change]
**Forbidden moves:** [what Editor must not create, change, or imply]
**Acceptance criteria:** [three to five observable checks]
**Requested output:** [final text plus one to five change notes, or an explicit user override]
```

## Rules

- Include only resolved sources and bounded editorial decisions.
- Make the handoff executable without exposing or duplicating Editor's internal canon.
- Convert a safe assumption into an explicit brief field; return an unsafe choice to the adaptive gate.
- Forbid unconfirmed facts, claims, proof, CTA, benefits, objections, guarantees, urgency, scarcity, offers, metrics, research, and marketing strategy.
- Do not include repository paths, selected references, canon references, or internal Editor mechanics.

**Complete when:** `a1-editor` can execute the assignment from the source material, operation label, allowed moves, forbidden moves, and acceptance criteria without making a marketing decision.
