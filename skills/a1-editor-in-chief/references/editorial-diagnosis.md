# Editorial Diagnosis

Diagnose what the supplied text must do better inside confirmed product and marketing inputs.

## Allowed Decisions

Decide only:

- which confirmed reader state matters most;
- which confirmed message deserves emphasis;
- the editorial angle on that message;
- the hierarchy of existing arguments;
- the text structure;
- the voice.

Do not create missing product truth, positioning, segmentation, pricing, offer, campaign, GTM, research, proof, or business strategy. If diagnosis reveals that one of those decisions is absent, return to the scope boundary instead of filling it.

## Operation Selection

Choose one primary `a1-editor` operation from the desired editorial result: standard edit, shorten, clarify, strengthen, restructure, or information style. Treat explanation and variants as output modifiers. Information style requires the user's explicit request.

Use only the operation label in the brief. Do not restate Editor's execution rules or load its internal references; Editor owns their meaning and application.

Turn the diagnosis into three to five observable acceptance criteria for the Editor result. Criteria must protect confirmed facts and constraints while describing the editorial improvement.

**Complete when:** the allowed editorial decisions, one primary Editor operation, and observable acceptance criteria are fixed without an unconfirmed marketing decision.
