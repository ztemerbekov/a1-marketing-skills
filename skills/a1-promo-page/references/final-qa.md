# Promo-Page Final QA

Run this check against the complete result.

## Offer and Audience

- The page presents one offer to one primary audience with one principal next action.
- The opening names a clear category and supported reader relevance.
- Each block answers a distinct buying question and adds new information.
- Secondary audiences or features do not displace the primary case.

## Claims and Evidence

- Every claim, comparison, testimonial, result, price, deadline, and guarantee traces to [source-policy.md](source-policy.md).
- Feature-to-outcome connections preserve conditions and actual contribution.
- Important promises have evidence or an explicit source gap.
- Visual notes describe evidence-bearing content rather than decoration.

## Fit and Transaction

- The reader can determine who the offer suits, what it includes, and what limitations matter.
- Price or the supported pricing process is represented honestly.
- Urgency, scarcity, discounts, eligibility, and guarantees are real and complete.
- The CTA matches the actual next step and explains what follows.

## Prose and Tone

- Copy is natural and specific to this offer.
- Headings communicate meaning instead of announcing importance.
- Tone supports a transparent peer relationship rather than pressure or synthetic intimacy.
- Technical and legal terms remain precise and understandable for the intended reader.

## Output Integrity

- Blueprint and Draft modes follow `../SKILL.md`.
- Content requirements and source gaps remain outside publishable copy.
- Artifact-only requests contain only the requested artifact.

Revise until every applicable check passes. If a central offer fact or proof item is missing, return the strongest honest blueprint or partial draft and identify the exact input needed rather than filling the page with generic claims.
