# Landing Page Copy Source Policy

This file owns the content boundary for `a1-landing-page-copy`.

## Allowed Sources

Use only:

1. Product, offer, audience, channel, and action inputs explicitly supplied in the current request.
2. Product documentation, approved claims, research, plans, prices, terms, screenshots, testimonials, cases, or other materials selected by the user.
3. Clearly applicable facts supplied earlier in the conversation.
4. The first eligible repository Marketing Context selected by `SKILL.md`.

Treat requests such as `make this irresistible`, `position us as the best`, or `sound premium` as creative direction, not proof.

## Offer Map

Separate the sources into:

- product category and what the buyer receives;
- primary audience and buying situation;
- desired next action;
- features and mechanisms;
- reader outcomes explicitly supported by those mechanisms;
- evidence: demonstrations, examples, cases, data, policies, or guarantees;
- price, package, timing, eligibility, delivery, and other transaction terms;
- fit limits, risks, and exclusions;
- approved testimonials and comparisons;
- visual or interactive assets already available;
- claims or blocks that still need support.

Every specific claim, outcome, comparison, price, result, quotation, testimonial, client name, guarantee, and deadline in the page must trace to this map.

## Claim Strength

Connect features to reader consequences only when the source supports the connection. A feature may make an outcome possible; it does not prove that every buyer will achieve that outcome.

Preserve conditions and uncertainty. `Can`, `may`, `up to`, eligibility rules, dependencies, and typical rather than guaranteed results must remain visible.

Replace unsupported evaluation with supported material. If the sources contain no proof for `best`, `fastest`, `effortless`, `trusted`, or an equivalent claim, omit it rather than decorating it.

## Testimonials, Cases, and Comparisons

Never invent a testimonial, customer, case, result, rating, credential, or quotation. Use supplied testimonials at their actual level of detail and attribution.

Do not turn a customer story into a general product guarantee. Preserve the customer's conditions and the product's actual contribution.

Compare alternatives only on sourced dimensions. Do not create an imaginary competitor, distorted baseline, or unsupported market position.

## Pricing and Urgency

Use only supplied prices, discounts, deadlines, inventory, eligibility, and guarantee terms. Represent the full conditions close to the claim.

Use truthful urgency. A real enrollment deadline, production slot, inventory limit, or time-bound price may be stated directly. Never create a countdown, expiring discount, limited quantity, or waiting list to pressure the reader.

## Marketing Context

Treat audience, Brand Voice, positioning, and business-goal entries as defaults that the current page may override. Treat product facts, claims and evidence, and explicit prohibitions as protected inputs.

When a current request conflicts with protected material, preserve the protected fact and surface the conflict. Ask one concise question only when every accurate page would require choosing which protected fact or term is current.

## Missing Material

Missing optional context does not block a useful blueprint or partial draft. Surface a gap when the page needs a price, package, proof item, eligibility rule, CTA destination, legal condition, or visual demonstration that the sources do not provide.

Keep unresolved inputs outside publishable copy. Use descriptive placeholders only when the user explicitly asks for a working template; make each placeholder name the exact fact needed.
